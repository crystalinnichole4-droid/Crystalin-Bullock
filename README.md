# Crystalin Bullock

Crystalin Bullock is a weird little world of cursed tokens, broken apps, and chaotic collectible lore.

Each token is a character living in a land where failed software and bad decisions coexist. People buy the tokens without knowing what they are, scan the QR code to reveal the character, and then that character becomes part of the town map.

The world keeps expanding as more tokens are purchased. Each token has a weird backstory, a strange defect, and a small scene that makes the land feel bigger and more unstable.

## The core idea

- Each token is a failed app turned into a character.
- Each token has a QR code that unlocks the story.
- The purchase is blind: you do not know what token is inside.
- Once scanned, the character appears in the world map.
- The town grows and mutates as more tokens are revealed.
- Seasonal chaos is part of the world: on December 1, everyone gets an ugly Christmas sweater.
- Some characters are comedic, some are unsettling, and some are absurdly beautiful in a broken way.

## Character focus

- Miner Man: grunts through the mine while crypto flies overhead.
- Miner Matt: digs in stupid directions and treats junk as treasure.
- The Magnet: failed Bluetooth app with everything stuck to it.
- GPS Ghost: obsessive map-following glitch.
- Oregon Trail Ghost: obsolete pixelated wagon through the town.
- Dinner Family: family eating dinner, staring in silence.
- Blockchain Bingo: a bingo room interrupted by contract panic.
- Lost Human: the only human in the land, surrounded by scaffold crews building houses around him.
- Fido Dino: a tiny cute-terrifying dino mascot who wandered out of a compromised app.
- Hidden Cave Characters: old signs, forgotten silhouettes, and weird treasure pits.

## Miner Matt lore

Miner Matt is a singular disaster.

He is constantly surrounded by gorgeous jewels — rubies, sapphires, emeralds, diamonds — all of them obvious treasure. He sees none of it. He is way more excited by a rusty bit of string, a broken bottle cap, or a scrap of old metal. He treats worthless junk like sacred treasure and never realizes what real value looks like.

His cave is filled with old signs, faded silhouettes, cracked billboards, and a weird collection of abandoned visual language. He thinks the old silhouettes are cool and meaningful, even though he has no idea what they were for. He keeps them like trophies, stacked in his cave like weird sacred junk.

## Fido Dino lore

Fido Dino is a failed little mascot app that somehow became alive. He is cute, tiny, and deeply weird. He wanders around the town with confused little dino sounds, always too excited, too innocent, and too wrong for the world he lives in.

He isn’t evil. He’s just one of the many failed things that made it out of the code and into the land.
